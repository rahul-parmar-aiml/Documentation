## The Four Approaches — Ranked Easiest to Most Detailed

### Approach 1 — Photo → AI Redesign (15–30 min, free to try)

Upload a photo of your current room, pick a style, get a transformed image in ~30 seconds.

**Best tools:**

**REimagineHome** (`reimaginehome.ai`)
- Best overall for most people
- 80+ design styles; handles wall colour, plants, furniture, decor
- "Real Product Staging" links AI-generated furniture to actual shoppable items with prices
- ~30-second renders; preserves room geometry better than competitors
- Free: 5 credits on signup | $14/mo (20 credits) → $99/mo (1,200 credits)
- Sweet spot: $29/mo tier for casual use

**Remodel AI** (`remodelai.io`)
- 30+ styles; includes wall paint and floor replacement
- More consistent than RoomGPT (useful results ~75–80% of the time)
- Free: 3 designs | Paid from $0–$29/mo

**RoomGPT** (`roomgpt.io`)
- Simplest of all — upload, pick style, done in 30 seconds
- Only ~10 styles; no layout editing
- Free: 3 credits | $9/mo (30) → $29/mo (200)

**HomeDesigns AI** (`homedesigns.ai`)
- Covers interiors, exteriors, gardens, wall colour in one place
- From $27/mo

**Honest caveats:**
- Furniture placed is AI-invented — you cannot click and buy it (except REimagineHome's shoppable tier)
- Wall colours are directionally suggestive, not paint-chip accurate — always test physical samples
- AI does not respect your exact room dimensions or structural constraints (outlets, radiators, doorways)
- Best used for deciding your *style direction*, not for purchasing decisions

### Approach 2 — AR: Place Virtual Furniture in Your Real Room (minutes, free)

Point your phone camera at the room, tap to place a virtual item, see it overlaid at real scale.

**Best tools:**

**IKEA Kreativ** (IKEA app — free)
- Scan your room with your phone; app builds a 3D replica with accurate dimensions
- Digitally remove your existing furniture, then refurnish from scratch with IKEA products
- ~98% size/scale accuracy; real lighting and shadows
- Limitation: IKEA catalog only

**Houzz "View in My Room 3D"** (Houzz app — free)
- Best multi-brand AR catalog (hundreds of designer brands)
- Visual Match AI: photograph any inspiration image, it surfaces similar purchasable items
- Supports panoramic 360° room previews

**Wayfair AR** (Wayfair app — free)
- Thousands of Wayfair products in 3D
- Good for Wayfair shoppers

**Amazon "View in Your Room"** (Amazon Shopping app — free)
- Multi-item placement; vast Amazon catalog

**Honest caveats:**
- You cannot design a whole room across brands in one app
- Best for confirming 2–3 specific items you are already considering before buying
- Low-light rooms cause scale and placement errors

### Approach 3 — Measure → Floor Plan → 3D Render (weekend project, mostly free)

Build your apartment to-scale digitally, furnish it with real-sized virtual furniture, generate photorealistic renders.

**Step 1 — Capture your room dimensions:**

| Tool | Method | Cost | Requires |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MagicPlan** (`magicplan.app`) | LiDAR room scan | Free basic / $25/project | iPhone 12 Pro+ or iPad Pro |
| **RoomScan Pro** (`roomscan.com`) | LiDAR tap-wall scan | ~$5–10 one-time | iPhone (iOS only) |
| **CubiCasa** (`cubi.casa`) | Photo walk of apartment | Free (1 plan) | Any smartphone |
| **Tape measure** | Manual | Free | Patience |

> **LiDAR tip:** If you have an iPhone 12 Pro/Max or newer, use MagicPlan or RoomScan Pro. It scans a full apartment in 30–60 minutes with high accuracy. No LiDAR? Enter measurements manually into the design tool.

**Step 2 — Build the floor plan and furnish:**

**Coohom** (`coohom.com`) ⭐ Best free option
- Drag-and-drop floor plan editor; 600,000+ 3D furniture models including real brands
- AI auto-suggests furniture placement
- Physically-based rendering (accurate shadows, material textures, window light) — free tier
- Renders up to 12K on paid plans; renders in ~10 seconds
- Free tier covers the full workflow | Pro $9–30/mo

**Homestyler** (`homestyler.com`)
- 1 million+ furniture items from 200+ real brands (IKEA, Wayfair, West Elm)
- AI layout generation from sketches
- Mobile AR preview: see your 3D design overlaid in your actual room
- Unlimited 1K renders free (watermarked) | Pro from ~$9.90/mo (4K, no watermark)

**Planner 5D** (`planner5d.com`)
- Best free multi-room floor planning tool
- AI can generate full 2D and 3D layouts automatically
- Solid mobile AR mode
- Free tier (watermarked) | Paid from ~$33–50/mo for HD renders

**RoomSketcher** (`roomsketcher.com`)
- Clean, beginner-friendly; 2D drawing auto-generates 3D simultaneously
- AI Convert: upload a photo of an existing floor plan → editable 3D project in 5 seconds
- Free basic | Pro ~$49/year

**Honest caveats:**
- Getting a usable floor plan right takes 2–6 hours if measuring manually; 30–60 min with LiDAR
- Populating furniture takes another 2–4 hours for a full apartment
- This is the only DIY approach that gives you spatially accurate results — worth the time if buying large furniture

### Approach 4 — Hybrid Workflow (full weekend, $0–$29/mo)

Combines all three approaches for the highest quality result. Approaches professional-grade output.

**The workflow:**

1. **Scan/measure** → MagicPlan or RoomScan Pro (30–60 min)
2. **Build floor plan + furnish** → Coohom or Homestyler (2–4 hours)
   - Place furniture at real scale; get spatially accurate renders
3. **AI style transformation** → REimagineHome on top of your renders (30 min)
   - Push your renders into an AI tool to explore wall colours, decor, lighting styles
   - Use shoppable bundles to find real purchasable versions of what the AI suggests
4. **AR confirmation** → IKEA Kreativ or Houzz for your final shortlisted items (30 min)
   - See specific chosen items in your actual room before buying

**Why this beats any single tool:** Step 2 gives spatial accuracy. Step 3 gives aesthetic exploration speed. Step 4 gives purchasing confidence. Total cost: free to $29/mo.

## AI Image Generation Tools (Creative / Power Users)

For generating photorealistic concept images of what your apartment *could* look like — not a planning tool, but excellent for style inspiration boards.

**Midjourney V7** (`midjourney.com`)
- Best pure image quality available; V7 (April 2025) has strong spatial coherence
- **Re-Texture mode**: upload your actual room photo → AI changes surfaces (floors, walls, fabric) while preserving room geometry
- Use for: "what if I had dark parquet floors and sage green walls?"
- Prompting tip: be very specific — "cerulean velvet sectional, warm recessed lighting, herringbone oak floors, morning light from west-facing window, --ar 16:9 --v 7"
- Cannot place specific furniture or respect exact dimensions
- No free tier | $10/mo (Basic) → $60/mo (Pro)

**ChatGPT with GPT-4o** (`chat.openai.com`)
- Conversational iteration: "make the sofa navy, add a floor lamp in the left corner, now change the walls to warm white"
- Upload your room photo and describe changes
- Lower image quality than Midjourney but the conversation interface is much more accessible
- Included in ChatGPT Plus $20/mo

**Adobe Firefly + Photoshop Generative Fill**
- Best for targeted element replacement in your actual room photo
- Select just the floor → type "herringbone oak parquet" → generates 3 variations blending perfectly with your lighting
- Also great for: removing clutter, adding rugs/artwork/plants, changing wall paint finish
- Works within Photoshop; licensed on Adobe Stock content (commercially clean)
- $19.99/mo (Creative Cloud Photography) or Firefly standalone from free (25 credits/mo)

**Stable Diffusion + ControlNet** (free, open source, technical)
- Most control of any tool: feed your room photo through edge/depth detection → AI is constrained to your actual room geometry
- ControlNet models for rooms: Canny (edges), MLSD (straight lines/walls), Depth (spatial depth)
- Inpaint any specific zone: mask just the floor, prompt "herringbone parquet" → only that area regenerates
- Requires: GPU with 8GB+ VRAM (local) or cloud platform ($10–$30/mo)
- Steep learning curve; not for non-technical users

## What AI Can and Cannot Do — Honest Summary

| Task | AI Can Do This? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Generate style inspiration images | ✅ Excellent | Midjourney, ChatGPT, REimagineHome |
| Visualise wall colour changes | ✅ Good | Directional only — always test physical paint samples |
| Furnish an empty room from a photo | ✅ Good | Results convincing at a glance; scale/placement may be off |
| Show plants and decor in a space | ✅ Good | Best via Homestyler or Coohom's catalog |
| Maintain consistent 3D model across views | ❌ No | Use Coohom/Homestyler for this instead |
| Respect your exact room dimensions | ❌ No | Only 3D floor plan tools do this |
| Place a specific named sofa in a specific corner | ❌ No | AR apps (IKEA/Houzz) handle individual items |
| Generate shoppable furniture lists | ⚠️ Partial | REimagineHome Pro does this; others approximate |
| Account for outlets, radiators, doors | ❌ No | You must check this manually |
| Show consistent lighting across day/night | ⚠️ Partial | Coohom handles this; AI photo tools do not |

## Full-Service Options (Hire a Designer)

If you don't want to do it yourself:

**Havenly** (`havenly.com`)
- Human designer + AI tools
- Mini: $129/room | Full: $199/room (concept boards, product suggestions, layout, revisions)
- Max room size 400 sq ft per package; turnaround 1–2 weeks

**Decorilla** (`decorilla.com`)
- Two designers compete with concepts; you choose your favourite
- Includes up to 45% discounts at 300+ furniture vendors (can offset the fee)
- From $449/room | Turnaround 2–4 weeks

**Smart approach:** Use AI tools first (free) to clarify your style direction, then hire a designer only if you need structural changes, contractor management, or custom work. This reportedly saves 40–60% of design hours.

## Recommended Workflows by Goal

**"I just want to see what my apartment could look like in a different style"**
→ Upload photos to REimagineHome (5 free renders). Pick 3 styles you like. Takes 15 minutes.

**"I'm buying a sofa and want to see if it fits and looks right"**
→ Use Houzz "View in My Room 3D" or IKEA Kreativ (if IKEA). See it at real scale in your actual room. Free.

**"I'm planning a significant redecoration and want to plan furniture layout before buying anything large"**
→ Spend a weekend in Coohom (free). Scan with MagicPlan if you have a qualifying iPhone. Build your apartment to scale, furnish it, iterate until the layout works. Then use REimagineHome on your renders for style tweaks.

**"I want the most realistic possible result, as close to what it will actually look like"**
→ Full hybrid workflow: MagicPlan scan → Coohom floor plan + furnish → REimagineHome style pass → AR confirmation of final items. Total: ~$0–$29/mo, one full weekend.

**"I want creative / photorealistic inspiration images, not a floor plan"**
→ Midjourney V7 ($10/mo) with detailed prompts, or ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) for conversational iteration on your own room photos.

## Key Rules Before You Start

1. **Never buy large furniture based solely on AI renders.** Always cross-check dimensions with a tape measure.
2. **Always test physical paint samples** on your actual wall in your actual lighting — AI wall colour is directional, not accurate.
3. **Check outlets, radiators, and door swing clearances** manually — no AI tool knows these.
4. **Start free.** Every recommended tool above has a meaningful free tier. Spend money only if you decide you need more renders or higher resolution.
